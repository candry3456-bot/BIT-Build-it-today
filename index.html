THE STORY

WHERE DID
BIT COME FROM?

“But how did you start?”

During our activity in robotics, two children once asked us how we had started working in technology.

We told them that we had discovered CAD and started learning by ourselves.

We searched the internet. We watched tutorials. We tried. We failed. We tried again.

At first, it was difficult.

But eventually, we learned more, built more and became better.

The children asked us this question because they simply didn't know where to start.

Not every student lacks curiosity.
Not every student lacks motivation.
Sometimes, they simply need someone to show them where to begin.

That is where BIT came from.

BIT was created from the idea that every student should have the opportunity to discover technology, learn practical skills and receive guidance from people who can help them take their first steps.

“Everyone should have the opportunity to learn.”
